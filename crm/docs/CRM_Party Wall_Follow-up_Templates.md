Party Wall Notice – CRM Follow-Up Templates 

These templates are for use within the Rosecrest Group Ltd CRM Party Wall funnel. They are designed to be sent where a prospect has submitted a Party Wall Notice request via the website but has not responded to the initial automated email. 

Shape 

Week 1 Follow-Up Email 

Subject: Follow-up: Your free Party Wall Notice 

Hi {{contact.first_name}}, 

We hope you’re well. 

We’re just following up regarding your recent Party Wall Notice request submitted through our website. We haven’t yet received a response and wanted to check whether you require any assistance so we can proceed without delay. 

As a reminder, your free Party Wall Notice can be prepared once we have received the following: 

• A signed Letter of Appointment confirming your authorisation for Rosecrest Group Ltd to prepare the notice on your behalf. Click here to complete 
• Any technical drawings or architectural plans showing the proposed works 
• Land Registry title documents (if available) for your property and any adjoining properties 

If any of these were not uploaded during your online submission, please email them to rge@rosecrestgroupltd.co.uk. 

Once all documentation has been received, we will prepare your notice in accordance with the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 and issue an electronic copy for your review and use. Please note that responsibility for serving the notice on the adjoining owner(s) rests with the building owner. 

If the adjoining owner(s) consent, no further action is usually required. Should a dissent arise, Rosecrest Group Ltd would act as your appointed surveyor under the following fee structure: 

• Single Party Wall: £750 + VAT 
• Each additional adjoining owner: £350 + VAT 
• Joint Party Wall: £950 + VAT 
• Each additional adjoining owner: £350 + VAT 

If you have any questions or would like to confirm how best to proceed, please feel free to reply to this email or contact our team on 020 4576 5317.
